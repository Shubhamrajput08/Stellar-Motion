# Stellar-Motion
An star's spectrum can be viewed by plotting the intensity of starlight observed at each wavelength. Hydrogen, major component in stars, absorbs light at 656.3 nm and causes a dip in the spectrum. If the star is moving, the spectrum's dip is observed at a different wavelength. This difference is used to measure the direction and velocity of a star.
In this project, I am going to find out the direction and velocity of HD94028 (a faint star in the consetellation of Leo) when its moving.
